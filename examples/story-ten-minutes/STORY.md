# Story: Ten minutes

The motion-story skill's own demo, rendered from `templates/film.html` as it ships.

## Premise

You just robbed a bank and wait for your partner at a motel; he is late, the news says a suspect is in custody, and two texts tell you opposite things.

Fiction: every person, place and event is invented (Marco, the bank, the motel, the bulletin). Voice: Kokoro `am_michael`, synthetic, made on the author's machine with `npx hyperframes tts` (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0).

## The question

| | Answer | Why a viewer would pick it (planted in scene) |
|---|---|---|
| A | STAY | Marco's own text says the first one is a lie and he is five minutes away (TEXT2); the unknown number could be the police bluffing |
| B | RUN | the news says a suspect is in custody (NEWS), and Marco has not answered a call in half an hour (WAIT, CALL) |

## Never on screen

- A real bank, city, person or news channel; how the robbery was done; weapons.

## Music and sound

- The engine's bed in D minor, changing chord on every cut; a whoosh on each cut, data and a lock on the counters, pings and thuds on the texts, ticks on the countdown, a swell and a boom into the question. No sampled music. Normalised to -14 LUFS.
