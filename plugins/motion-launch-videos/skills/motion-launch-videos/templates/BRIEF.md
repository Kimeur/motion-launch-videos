# Brief: <product> launch bumper

Filled in by Claude from the user's answers and the product's own pages. Every line of copy in the film must trace back to a row in "Facts". With no live site (a demo, a fictional or unreleased product), the user's brief is the only source.

## Deliverable

| | |
|---|---|
| Product | <name>, <url> (or: no live site, demo / fictional / unreleased) |
